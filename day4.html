Problem Hunt

Define the Problem

Our evidenceUse these clues to fill in the “because.”
have a problem with because .
“with” = the symptomWhat we notice. It’s what goes wrong.
“because” = the causeWhy it happens. This is what a solution has to fix.

Kids have a problem with not having enough free time because their responsibilities and inefficient routines take up most of their afternoons.

Understand the problem first. Then decide how to solve it.

Solution Mapping

What Makes a Great Solution?

PROBLEM SOLVINGDoes it actually solve the problem?

It has to fix the cause, not just look cool.

DESIRABILITYWould people want to use it?

Kids and families have to like it enough to use it every day.

FEASIBILITYCould we realistically make it work?

Can it be built, with the money and tools we have?

🤔

Can an invention be really cool but still be a bad solution?

Creativity matters. But the best business ideas also solve a real customer need.

Can You Give Kids Back 60 Minutes a Day?

Your mission

Our problem

  1. The rules
  2. You can’t eliminate school, homework, sleep, family time, or responsibilities.
  3. You can invent a physical product, service, technology, or new system.
  4. Your solution must address the problem we identified together.
  5. Be creative and think big.
  6. You must explain how your invention works.
  7. It should save time, not just move the work to somebody else.
