What is a stock?

A stock is a tiny piece of a company.

Whole pizza = the companyYour slice = your share

Public

Anyone can buy a piece on the stock market.

  • Apple
  • Nike
  • Disney
  • McDonald’s

Private

Owned by founders or a small group. You can’t buy shares.

  • Chick-fil-A
  • LEGO
  • Epic Games (Fortnite)
  • Mars (M&M’s)
